Audi AG is a premier German luxury automobile manufacturer headquartered in Ingolstadt, Germany, and operates as a key subsidiary of the Volkswagen Group.

The Range Rover is the flagship sub-brand of ultra-luxury SUVs produced by the British automaker Jaguar Land Rover (JLR), which has been fully owned by India's Tata Motors since 2008.
